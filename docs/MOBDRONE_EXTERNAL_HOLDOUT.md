# MOBDrone external target-only holdout

## Decision

The frozen external evaluation is complete. Crop4's target representation transfers to independent
MOBDrone source videos when the frames are presented through location-blind tiles, but the tested
tiling arms are not deployable proposal rules: their target-class false-positive load and negative-
image trigger rate are high. No checkpoint, threshold, tile size, merge rule, or inference arm is
selected from these results.

Full-frame Repeat4 remains stronger than full-frame Crop4 on the mapped target endpoint. Blind
tiling reverses that relationship: Crop4-384 and Crop4-768 recover 188/350 and 132/350 lifebuoys,
whereas Repeat4-384 and Repeat4-768 recover 0/350 and 2/350. This supports a representation/
presentation mechanism for Crop4, not a completed rare-class repair.

## Dataset and frozen role

[MOBDrone 1.0.0](https://aimh.isti.cnr.it/dataset/mobdrone/) contains 126,170 annotated frames,
181,689 boxes, and five classes. The official recommended test prefix is `DJI_0804`; the dataset is
released under Creative Commons Attribution and archived as
[Zenodo record 5996890](https://zenodo.org/records/5996890), DOI
[`10.5281/zenodo.5996890`](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5996890). Only the comparable endpoint is
mapped:

```text
MOBDrone life_buoy -> SeaDronesSee life_saving_appliances
```

No other class mapping is used for a headline metric. In particular, `person` is not treated as
`swimmer`, and no cross-dataset aggregate mAP is reported.

The evaluation rule was frozen before video download or inference in
[`configs/mobdrone_external_holdout.yaml`](../configs/mobdrone_external_holdout.yaml). Within every
official `DJI_0804` source video, annotated frames were sorted and the first plus every 30th
subsequent frame was retained. The resulting fixed-rate holdout contains 1,264 frames from 26
source videos, including 350 lifebuoy boxes in 349 target-positive frames and 915 target-negative
frames. Sixteen source videos contain at least one target. Normalized source-name overlap with all
SeaDronesSee roles is zero.

## Frozen inference matrix

Both frozen `best.pt` checkpoints were evaluated at confidence floor 0.001 and IoU 0.70. Full-frame
inference used `imgsz=1280`. Blind 384- and 768-pixel tiles used 25% overlap, were resized to 1280
for inference, and were merged by class-aware NMS at IoU 0.70. All six arms are reported.

| Condition | Target matches / 350 | Recall @ IoU 0.50 | Target AP50 | Target AP50-95 | Target FP / image | Negative frames triggered |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Repeat4 full frame | 44 | 0.1257 | 0.0791 | 0.0241 | 0.0854 | 2.62% |
| Crop4 full frame | 30 | 0.0857 | 0.0346 | 0.0072 | 0.3544 | 10.82% |
| Repeat4 tile 384 | 0 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 15.8402 | 62.95% |
| Repeat4 tile 768 | 2 | 0.0057 | 0.0013 | 0.0003 | 1.0941 | 26.45% |
| Crop4 tile 384 | 188 | 0.5371 | 0.3055 | 0.0805 | 7.9794 | 81.09% |
| Crop4 tile 768 | 132 | 0.3771 | 0.2186 | 0.0689 | 3.4098 | 60.11% |

The 300-detection cap does not hide additional target matches: the retained uncapped target streams
produce the same IoU-0.50 match counts as the primary streams for all tiled conditions. Across the
16 target-positive source videos, Crop4-384 and Crop4-768 match at least one target in 14 and 15
videos respectively. The transfer evidence is therefore distributed across sources, but so is the
false-positive burden.

## Interpretation and claim boundary

The external result strengthens the earlier mechanistic conclusion: Crop4 learned a recognizable
lifesaving-appliance representation that can be exposed by object-enlarging presentation, and that
behavior is not confined to SeaDronesSee detector-dev. It does not show that a frozen tiled system
is operationally useful. At the same low confidence floor, Crop4-384 triggers on 81.09% of target-
negative frames and produces 7.98 target-class false positives per image; Crop4-768 still triggers
on 60.11% of negatives and produces 3.41 target false positives per image.

These data are external-domain evidence, not a replacement for protected official validation.
They were not used for training, calibration fitting, threshold tuning, checkpoint selection, or
tile-size selection. Calibration-fit and policy-tune were not revisited, and official SeaDronesSee
validation was not run.

The next defensible step is method development with a newly frozen development role—for example,
a proposal-gating or hard-negative method—followed by a separately preregistered evaluation. It
must not tune on this completed MOBDrone holdout or retroactively select one of the six reported
arms.
